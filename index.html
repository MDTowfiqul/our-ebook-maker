How it works

  • Text PDFs are converted in seconds — the text is already inside the file.
  • Scanned PDFs are detected page by page. Each image-only page is read with OCR (Tesseract) in Bengali, English or Arabic, whichever you picked.
  • Chapter headings (Chapter, অধ্যায়, পরিচ্ছেদ, পর্ব, Part, etc.) become the ebook's table of contents automatically.
  • Nothing is uploaded. The PDF, the OCR and the EPUB building all happen in this browser tab. Close the tab and your file is gone.
Be honest about scanned books: old, faded or low-resolution scans will still have OCR mistakes, and curved phone-camera photos are the hardest of all — flat scans work best. That is why the 5-page test exists, and why the proofreading step stays part of making a good ebook. This tool does the heavy lifting; proofreading does the finishing.

Keep your screen on during a long OCR run — a phone that sleeps pauses the work. The tool tries to keep the screen awake while it runs.
