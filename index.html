4. Automatic proofreading (optional)

After OCR, proofread automatically right here instead of going to Gemini by hand. Your OpenRouter API key stays only in your browser — it goes to no server of ours, and we never see it. It is sent directly to OpenRouter.

If you have no key, create one from your OpenRouter account. The key is saved in your browser, so you won't need to type it again.

The text is sent in chunks with the same proofreading prompt, and the answers are joined into one proofread file. The cost comes from your OpenRouter account.
