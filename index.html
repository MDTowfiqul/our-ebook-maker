  1. For yourself only — you may convert any PDF book, old or new, no problem. Just do not share the finished ebook online if the book is less than 10 years old.
  2. For everyone, to share with everyone — you may share it only when the book is 10+ years old, OR the writer or publisher has published the PDF publicly themselves or has no problem with it being shared online.

When you have an ebook that may be shared, send it to the Our Boighar community (Facebook group / Telegram linked on the site's About page) so it can be checked and added to the library.

🤖 Automatic proofreading

After OCR, proofread automatically right here instead of by hand — with your own Google Gemini key or OpenRouter key. The key stays only in your browser — it goes to no server of ours, and we never see it. It is sent directly to Google or OpenRouter.

The text is sent in chunks (about 8,000 characters) with the same proofreading prompt, and the answers are joined into one proofread file. If a free key hits its daily limit, the tool waits and retries on its own, and progress is saved after every chunk — nothing is lost, and you can continue from the same place later. Gemini is free on a free key; on OpenRouter the cost comes from your account.

Book-like format: document and ebook outputs use a title page, chapters starting on new pages, centred headings, justified text (Arabic text right-aligned). For an exactly-like-print layout (drop caps, ornaments, special fonts), formatting by hand in Google Docs/Word after download is still best.

Which format, for which work?

  • EPUB — best for phones, tablets and almost every reading app (Apple Books, Google Play Books, Moon+ Reader, the Our Boighar reader).
  • PDF — layout stays exactly fixed — for printing or sharing, and for readers with no ebook app.
  • AZW3 — for Amazon Kindle devices and apps.
  • MOBI — for older Kindles.
  • KFX — the newest Kindle format, with enhanced typesetting.

1) Automatic — right here on this site

The document (.docx), EPUB and PDF are built right here in your browser — nothing has to be uploaded to any site.

2) Manual — with the online-convert website (for Kindle formats)

AZW3 / MOBI cannot be built directly in a browser (they are Amazon's own formats) — use online-convert for those. This site has no server, so a server-based conversion like online-convert's cannot run automatically here; this is the manual way.

  1. Download the .docx or EPUB automatically above.
  2. ebook.online-convert.com — open it, upload the file, and pick AZW3 (Kindle) or MOBI (older Kindle) as the output.
  3. Press Convert, then download the finished file.
🌐 Open online-convert

Make AZW3 / MOBI / KFX with Calibre:

  1. Download the EPUB with the EPUB button above.
  2. Calibre — install this free software on a computer.
  3. Add the EPUB to Calibre → select the book → “Convert books” → pick AZW3 (Kindle) or MOBI (older Kindle) as output format → OK.
  4. For KFX, add the “KFX Output” plugin to Calibre (Preferences → Plugins).

Tip: to send straight to a Kindle, EPUB itself can be sent with Send to Kindle — no conversion needed.
