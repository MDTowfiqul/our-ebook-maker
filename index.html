An ebook is made for 2 purposes:

  1. For yourself only — you may convert any PDF book, old or new, no problem. Just do not share the finished ebook online if the book is less than 10 years old.
  2. For everyone, to share with everyone — you may share it only when the book is 10+ years old, OR the writer or publisher has published the PDF publicly themselves or has no problem with it being shared online.

When you have an ebook that may be shared, send it to the Our Boighar community (Facebook group / Telegram linked on the site's About page) so it can be checked and added to the library.

4. Automatic proofreading (optional)

After OCR, proofread automatically right here instead of by hand — with your own Google Gemini key or OpenRouter key. The key stays only in your browser — it goes to no server of ours, and we never see it. It is sent directly to Google or OpenRouter.

The text is sent in chunks (about 8,000 characters) with the same proofreading prompt, and the answers are joined into one proofread file. If a free key hits its daily limit, the tool waits and retries on its own, and progress is saved after every chunk — nothing is lost, and you can continue from the same place later. Gemini is free on a free key; on OpenRouter the cost comes from your account.
