Scanned PDF in , clean ebook out

Muse reads your PDF (Bijoy included), proofreads it, and hands you a formatted Google Doc — then this site turns that Doc into EPUB and Kindle formats.

⚠️ Before you make an ebook — read this first

First, check whether the book is already on Our Boighar:

🔍 Search Our Boighar — if the book is already there, there is no need to make it again. (If you still want your own copy, you can make it anyway.)

An ebook is made for 2 purposes:

  1. For yourself only — you may convert any PDF book, old or new, no problem. Just do not share the finished ebook online if the book is less than 10 years old.
  2. For everyone, to share with everyone — you may share it only when the book is 10+ years old, OR the writer or publisher has published the PDF publicly themselves or has no problem with it being shared online.

When you have an ebook that may be shared, send it to the Our Boighar community (Facebook group / Telegram linked on the site's About page) so it can be checked and added to the library.

1. Your book

Your scanned PDF does not go here — it goes to the Muse chat in Step 2. Here you only set the book title, author, cover and your name — those are what go into the finished ebook.

Book cover (optional)

You will need a cover when you make the final ebook. If you already have a cover, upload it. If not, find one online or make a text cover below. If you don't like a text-cover style, tap the same button again — there is no limit, every tap makes a new design.

Tap “Use as cover” on any cover below and it goes straight into your final ebook — no need to download and upload it again.

Swipe left/right to browse the covers, then tap “Use as cover” on the one you like.

2. Muse route — scanned PDF to Google Doc (the easy way)

This site itself cannot call Muse — that part happens in a Muse chat, and this site does the steps after it. So this route needs no browser OCR, no Resume, and no API key.

Muse is not directly available in Bangladesh — you open an account with a USA VPN.

  1. Install a USA VPN — I use Green VPN: Green VPN on Google Play — connect to a USA server.
  2. Follow the steps in this video to create your Muse account: Video tutorial
  3. Then come back here and follow the Muse route above.
  1. Open a Muse chat and attach your scanned PDF — a scanned PDF of an old, copyright-free book. Even legacy Bijoy/SutonnyMJ fonts are fine — Muse converts them to Unicode Bangla.
  2. Paste the full prompt — read it with the “Read & copy the prompt” button below, copy it from there, and paste it in the same message.
  3. Take the Google Doc link — at the end Muse builds a formatted Google Doc and sends you the link. The Doc begins directly with the book title — no cover-page or artwork notes.
  4. Download the .docx from the Doc and upload it in Step 3 — open the Google Doc, go to File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx), then put that file in Step 3's box — EPUB and the Kindle path open from there.

The sharing rules (⚠️ box above) apply to this route too.

3. Final ebook — from document to ebook

Download the .docx from your Google Doc and upload it below — that unlocks the EPUB button.

📄 Your Google Doc (.docx)

Open your Google Doc, go to File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx), then upload that file here.

  • EPUB — best for phones, tablets and almost every reading app (Apple Books, Google Play Books, Moon+ Reader, the Our Boighar reader).
  • AZW3 — for Amazon Kindle devices and apps.
  • MOBI — for older Kindles.
  • KFX — the newest Kindle format, with enhanced typesetting.

1) EPUB — right here on this site

EPUB is built right here in your browser — nothing has to be uploaded to any site.

2) Kindle formats (AZW3 / MOBI) — with the online-convert website

AZW3 / MOBI cannot be built directly in a browser (they are Amazon's own formats) — use online-convert for those. This site has no server, so a server-based conversion like online-convert's cannot run here.

⚠️ Make the EPUB first, then convert it to Kindle

Download the EPUB from option 1 above, then upload that EPUB file to online-convert — do not upload the .docx directly. The cover photo and credit page are added when the EPUB is built; a Kindle file made straight from the .docx will have neither. For KFX via Calibre, convert from the EPUB too, not the .docx.

  1. Use the EPUB you downloaded from option 1 above.
  2. ebook.online-convert.com — open it, upload the file, and pick AZW3 (Kindle) or MOBI (older Kindle) as the output.
  3. Press Convert, then download the finished file.

📘 Guide — how to make an ebook

  1. Your book (Step 1): title, author, cover and your name for the credit page. Your scanned PDF does not go here.
  2. Muse route (Step 2): attach the scanned PDF in a Muse chat, paste the full prompt, and take the Google Doc link — Muse finishes the whole book automatically. Muse handles Bijoy conversion and proofreading.
  3. Final ebook (Step 3): download the .docx from the Google Doc (File → Download → Microsoft Word), upload it here, and download EPUB. AZW3/MOBI via online-convert, KFX via Calibre.
  4. Before you share: check Our Boighar first. For yourself only, any book is fine; to share, it must be 10+ years old or writer/publisher-approved.

Privacy policy

This site has no server, no account, and nothing is uploaded. Reading the .docx and building EPUB all happen in your browser tab. Your scanned PDF goes only to your own Muse chat — this site never sees it and never uploads it.
