📖 Our Ebook Maker — Full Guide

From a PDF to a clean, professional EPUB, in three steps. The tool on the home page replaces the old vFlat step — the rest of this guide is how a good ebook is finished.

Maker Full guide & proofreading Our Boighar

Step 1 — Get the text out (this site does it)

  1. Open the Maker, upload your PDF, pick the book's language, and press Test first 5 pages.
  2. If the preview reads cleanly, press Make the full EPUB. Text pages convert in seconds; scanned pages are read with OCR, page by page, so a full book can take a while — keep the screen on.
  3. You get two downloads: the EPUB itself, and the raw .txt. The .txt is for Step 2.

Flat scans work best. Curved phone-camera photos of pages are the hardest for any OCR, including this one. If a scan is very faded or blurry, no tool will read it perfectly — that is what proofreading is for.

You can also upload a .txt file directly (for example, one you already made with vFlat) and turn it into an EPUB here, skipping OCR entirely.

Step 2 — Proofreading (the step that makes it professional)

Open Google AI Studio, attach your .txt file chapter by chapter, and paste this prompt:

Then write continue and run again, chapter by chapter, until the whole book is done. Copy each response as Markdown. You can open several AI Studio tabs at once and proofread several books in parallel — it saves a lot of time.

Note: a PDF can also be attached directly to AI Studio for proofreading, but the licence text below must still appear somewhere in the book. A .docx file cannot be attached directly to AI Studio — use .txt or PDF.

Fonts that look like the original book: Noto Serif Bengali or SulaimanLipi. The EPUBs made here already use justified text, centred headings, and right-aligned Arabic.

Step 3 — Cover and finish

  • On this site: just add a cover image on the Maker page and it becomes the ebook's cover automatically.
  • Full-page cover trick (Google Docs route): tap the image, open Image options → Size & rotation → uncheck "lock aspect ratio", set position to "Fix position on page", then expand the image to fill the page.
  • After proofreading: save the proofread text as a .txt, upload it to the Maker, and download the finished EPUB. Check it opens in Google Play Books or any EPUB reader before sharing it.

One rule to keep in mind

Make and share ebooks only from books you have the right to share — old, copyright-free books, or books whose writer or publisher has approved sharing. That keeps the work honest and keeps writers and publishers unharmed.
