Proofreading Guide

From raw scanned text to a clean, professional ebook — step by step.

Proofread with Gemini — in 5 steps

  1. Download the .txt from the Maker (or use your own .txt).
  2. Open Google AI Studio (Gemini) and attach the .txt chapter by chapter.
  3. Paste the prompt below.
  4. After each answer, write “continue” and run again until the whole book is done.
  5. Copy each answer as Markdown. Then take it into Google Docs (Noto Serif Bengali / SulaimanLipi font), export as .docx, and use an online docx-to-EPUB converter for the final EPUB.

Tip: open several AI Studio tabs and proofread several books in parallel to save time. A PDF can also be attached directly to AI Studio, but the licence text must appear somewhere in the book. A .docx cannot be attached directly to AI Studio.

Cover and finishing

  • If the Maker found no cover, search online or make a text cover there, then use it in the final EPUB.
  • For a full-page cover in Google Docs: tap the image → Image options → uncheck “lock aspect ratio” under Size & rotation, set position to “Fix position on page”, then expand the image to fill the page.

One rule to keep in mind

Make and share ebooks only from books you have the right to share — old, copyright-free books, or books whose writer or publisher has approved sharing. That keeps writers and publishers unharmed.
