How to make an ebook — the full guide

From a scanned PDF book to a clean ebook — the full method in 5 parts. On the Maker the steps are numbered 1–3; Part 5 below is the Maker's final step (Step 3).

  1. 1 — Before you start: check Our Boighar, and the two purposes
  2. 2 — The Muse route: PDF to Google Doc (the only route)
  3. 3 — Cover and credit
  4. 4 — Review in Google Docs (optional, for an exactly-like-print look)
  5. 5 — Final formats: EPUB, AZW3, MOBI, KFX

Part 1 — Before you start

First, check whether the book is already on Our Boighar — if it is, there is no need to make it again. (If you still want your own copy, you can make it anyway.)

An ebook is made for 2 purposes:

  1. For yourself only — you may convert any PDF book, old or new, no problem. Just do not share the finished ebook online if the book is less than 10 years old.
  2. For everyone, to share with everyone — you may share it only when the book is 10+ years old, OR the writer or publisher has published the PDF publicly themselves or has no problem with it being shared online.

When you have an ebook that may be shared, send it to the Our Boighar community (Facebook group / Telegram linked on the site's About page) so it can be checked and added to the library.

Part 2 — The Muse route: PDF to Google Doc (the only route)

There is no browser OCR, no API key and no proofreading panel on this site anymore — everything up to the finished document happens in a Muse chat, with one prompt. The Maker's Step 2 has the full prompt behind its Copy button and pop-up.

  1. Open a Muse chat and attach your scanned PDF of an old, copyright-free book.
  2. Paste the full prompt from the Maker's Step 2 (the “Copy the full prompt” button) in the same message. It tells Muse to extract the text — converting legacy Bijoy/SutonnyMJ fonts to Unicode Bangla — proofread it in chunks, and build a formatted Google Doc.
  3. Muse works through the whole book automatically — it does not stop for permission between parts, so there is no DONE/continue round trip anymore. When the whole book is finished, Muse sends the Google Doc link and also gives you the finished document as a downloadable file (.docx) — not only the link.
  4. The finished document reads like a main published book: it begins directly with the book title, followed by the author, translator and publication information as printed in the original, then the table of contents (if the original has one), then the chapters in order.
  5. The Doc begins directly with the book title — no cover-page or artwork notes. Its formatting rules are inside the prompt: centred titles and headings, justified Bangla text, Arabic right-aligned with harkat, footnotes next to their numbers in superscript third brackets, and the author's wording left untouched.
  6. If Muse makes a mistake in a chunk, correct it in that same chat before moving on — the Doc is only as clean as the last chunk you accepted.

Part 3 — Cover and credit

Set these in the Maker's Step 1 before you build the final ebook:

  1. Upload a cover image if you have one — the uploaded cover is the one that goes into the final ebook. No cover? Find one online with the search button, or make a text cover — tap the same button again for a new design, every tap makes a new one. When a cover you like appears, tap “Use as cover” under it to set it for your final ebook.
  2. Put the ebook creator's name in Step 1's “Ebook creator name” field, right below Author — the credit page then reads “ইবুক নির্মাতা: <name>” followed by “সৌজন্যে: Our Ebook Maker ও Our Boighar”. The optional “Ebook creator link” box right below it (social media or contact) becomes the embedded link on the creator's name; leave it empty and the name stays plain text.
  3. The finished EPUB ends with an “About this ebook” credit page naming the ebook creator (your name, if given), Our Ebook Maker and Our Boighar.

Part 4 — Review in Google Docs (optional)

Before you download the .docx, you can check the Google Doc itself:

  1. Open the Doc Muse sent you and skim the chapter headings, the first pages of a few chapters, and any Arabic passages.
  2. Make any final hand formatting here (drop caps, ornaments, special fonts) — this is the only place an exactly-like-print layout can be made, because no automatic tool can do that decoration.
  3. Then download it: File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx), and upload that file in the Maker's Step 3.

Part 5 — Final formats

The Maker's Step 3: upload the .docx from your Google Doc. The EPUB-download button unlocks at once.

Tap “Which format, for which work?” in Step 3 to expand or collapse the format list.

The document is read (headings kept as chapters) and all formats below become available. Nothing is uploaded to any site — the .docx is read and the EPUB is built right here in your browser.

Which format, for which work?

  • EPUB — best for phones, tablets and almost every reading app (Apple Books, Google Play Books, Moon+ Reader, the Our Boighar reader).
  • AZW3 — for Amazon Kindle devices and apps.
  • MOBI — for older Kindles.
  • KFX — the newest Kindle format, with enhanced typesetting.

EPUB — right here on this site

EPUB is built in your browser from the uploaded .docx, with the title page, chapters on new pages, centred headings, justified text (Arabic right-aligned), your cover, and the credit page.

The EPUB embeds the Hind Siliguri font, so Bengali text shapes correctly even in a reader whose default font cannot shape it (Google Play Books' default font is the known case) — you do not need to change the reader's font for the Bengali to read correctly.

Kindle formats (AZW3 / MOBI) — with the online-convert website

AZW3 / MOBI cannot be built directly in a browser (they are Amazon's own formats) — use online-convert for those. This site has no server, so a server-based conversion like online-convert's cannot run here.

  1. Make the EPUB first, then convert it: use the EPUB built above (option 1) — not the .docx. The cover photo and credit page are added when the EPUB is built, so a Kindle file converted straight from the .docx will have neither. The same applies to KFX via Calibre: convert from the EPUB.
  2. ebook.online-convert.com — open it, upload the file, and pick AZW3 (Kindle) or MOBI (older Kindle) as the output.
  3. Press Convert, then download the finished file.

With Calibre (AZW3 / MOBI / KFX)

  1. Download the EPUB with the EPUB button in Step 3.
  2. Calibre — install this free software on a computer.
  3. Add the EPUB to Calibre → select the book → “Convert books” → pick AZW3 (Kindle) or MOBI (older Kindle) as output format → OK.
  4. For KFX, add the “KFX Output” plugin to Calibre (Preferences → Plugins).

Tip: to send straight to a Kindle, EPUB itself can be sent with Send to Kindle — no conversion needed.
